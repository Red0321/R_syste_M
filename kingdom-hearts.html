SUMMARY

Kingdom Hearts is a phenomenon repeatedly documented throughout the history of the worlds.

Its manifestations have been associated with hearts, worlds, light, darkness and the balance connecting them.

The phenomenon has appeared in multiple forms throughout history and has repeatedly become the center of conflicts involving Keyblade wielders.

The complete historical record extends from the age preceding the original Keyblade War through the events following the Second Keyblade War and the emergence of an unidentified reality known as Quadratum.

PHENOMENON RECORD

The exact nature of Kingdom Hearts remains incompletely understood.

Historical records describe multiple manifestations associated with different conflicts and different periods of history.

The phenomenon remains one of the central unresolved elements in the history of the worlds.

CURRENT STATUS

The most recent records connect the history of Kingdom Hearts to the aftermath of the Second Keyblade War and the events surrounding Sora's disappearance.

The subsequent appearance of QUADRATUM represents an unresolved continuation of these events.

RECORD STATUS

This record has been compiled from historical accounts concerning Kingdom Hearts and the individuals, locations, organizations and entities associated with the phenomenon.

The chronological framework is divided into periods surrounding the original Keyblade War and the Second Keyblade War.

Several historical records remain incomplete or classified.
