SUMMARY

Organization XIII was a clandestine organization composed primarily of individuals who had lost their hearts and continued to exist as Nobodies.

The organization became involved in a prolonged series of incidents concerning the Heartless, Keyblade wielders and the phenomenon known as Kingdom Hearts.

Its activities extended across multiple worlds and directly affected numerous individuals associated with the recorded conflicts.

KNOWN OBJECTIVE

Available records indicate that the organization sought to obtain hearts through the destruction of Heartless and use the resulting energy in the creation of Kingdom Hearts.

The organization's ultimate objective was connected to the restoration of its members' hearts and the completion of their existence.

DISSOLUTION

Organization XIII ceased to function as a unified organization following the destruction of its leadership and the collapse of its operations.

Several individuals previously associated with the organization subsequently reappeared under their original identities.

CURRENT STATUS

Organization XIII is considered disbanded.

Records indicate that several former members remain relevant to ongoing incidents concerning the Keyblade and the worlds.

RECORD STATUS

This record has been compiled from historical accounts concerning Organization XIII and its known activities.

Several portions of the organization's history remain incomplete or classified.
