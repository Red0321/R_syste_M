PROFILE

Baldr was a Keyblade wielder and student of MASTER ODIN at SCALA AD CAELUM .

He belonged to the generation of Keyblade wielders that included XEHANORT and ERAQUS .

Baldr possessed an unusual sensitivity to the hearts of other people, allowing him to perceive the emotions and darkness contained within them.

SCALA AD CAELUM
SCALA AD CAELUM

Baldr lived and trained in Scala ad Caelum as a member of Master Odin's class.

His sensitivity to the hearts of others distinguished him from the other students but also made him particularly vulnerable to the emotional effects of the darkness he perceived.

HODER
HODER

Hoder was Baldr's older sister and one of the upper-class Keyblade wielders of Scala ad Caelum.

Baldr was deeply attached to Hoder and relied on her emotional support.

Her death became the decisive event that allowed Darkness to exploit Baldr's grief.

DARKNESS

An incarnation of True Darkness exploited Baldr's grief following Hoder's death.

The Darkness gradually manipulated Baldr's emotions and eventually took control of his heart.

Baldr subsequently became the primary vessel through which the Darkness carried out its plan in Scala ad Caelum.

THE DISAPPEARANCES

A series of disappearances and deaths affected the Keyblade wielders of Scala ad Caelum.

Baldr's ability to perceive darkness within the hearts of others made him increasingly suspicious of those around him.

The resulting fear, grief and isolation further strengthened the Darkness within him.

DARK ROAD INCIDENTS
DARK ROAD INCIDENTS

Baldr became central to the investigation into the disappearances of the Keyblade wielders in Scala ad Caelum.

His own connection to the incidents remained concealed until the final stage of the investigation.

XEHANORT
XEHANORT

Xehanort was one of the Keyblade wielders who investigated the disappearances surrounding Scala ad Caelum.

His encounters with Baldr contributed to his growing understanding of the relationship between darkness and the human heart.

ERAQUS
ERAQUS

Eraqus participated with Xehanort in the investigation and later confronted Baldr.

Unlike Xehanort, Eraqus believed Baldr could potentially be separated from the Darkness without being killed.

THE THIRTEEN LIGHTS

Baldr's final objective was connected to the deaths of thirteen lights.

The Darkness intended to use the accumulated darkness generated by these deaths to summon Kingdom Hearts.

Baldr became the instrument through which this plan was carried out.

HRINGHORN

During the final stage of the conflict, the Darkness within Baldr manifested the enormous Heartless known as Hringhorn.

Hringhorn represented the overwhelming power of the Darkness controlling Baldr.

Xehanort and Eraqus defeated the creature before confronting Baldr directly.

KINGDOM HEARTS

Baldr intended to use the thirteen sacrifices to summon Kingdom Hearts.

The purpose of the ritual was to recreate the world according to the will of the Darkness.

The plan failed during the final confrontation.

BALDR CONFLICT
BALDR CONFLICT

Baldr was revealed as the central antagonist behind the deaths and disappearances that had affected Scala ad Caelum.

His body had become a vessel for Darkness, which manipulated his grief and memories.

FINAL BATTLE
DARK ROAD FINAL BATTLE

Xehanort and Eraqus confronted Baldr after defeating Hringhorn.

Eraqus attempted to save Baldr by separating him from the Darkness.

Xehanort ultimately defeated Baldr, bringing the immediate threat to Scala ad Caelum to an end.

HODER'S SPIRIT
HODER

Hoder's spirit appeared during the final confrontation.

Her presence revealed the emotional connection between Baldr's grief and the Darkness that had consumed him.

MASTER ODIN
ODIN

Master Odin was Baldr's instructor at Scala ad Caelum.

Odin intervened during the final conflict and helped bring the incident to its conclusion.

FINAL FATE

Baldr was killed during the final confrontation in Scala ad Caelum.

His death resulted in the destruction of the Darkness that had possessed him.

His spirit was subsequently reunited with Hoder and the other deceased Keyblade wielders.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Baldr's case became one of the defining incidents of Xehanort's youth.

His transformation demonstrated how grief and other negative emotions could provide Darkness with an opportunity to influence a person's heart.

The incident contributed directly to Xehanort's later interest in the nature of Darkness and the relationship between light and darkness.
