PROFILE

Gula was one of the five Foretellers appointed by the MASTER OF MASTERS during the period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

He was entrusted with leadership of the LEOPARDOS Union.

Unlike several of the other Foretellers, Gula became primarily concerned with discovering the identity of the traitor mentioned in the Master's instructions.

UNION ASSOCIATION
LEOPARDOS

Gula served as the Foreteller responsible for the Leopardos Union.

His Union formed one of the five principal organizations of Keyblade wielders operating before the Keyblade War.

BOOK OF PROPHECIES
BOOK OF PROPHECIES

Gula received a copy of the Book of Prophecies from the Master of Masters.

He became particularly interested in the Master's references to a traitor among the Foretellers.

THE LOST PAGE
LOST PAGE

Gula discovered that a page was missing from the Book of Prophecies.
