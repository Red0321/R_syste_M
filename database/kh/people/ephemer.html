PROFILE

Ephemer was a Keyblade wielder active during the period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

He was originally associated with DAYBREAK TOWN and became one of the most significant known survivors of the events that led to the destruction of the world.

Ephemer later became a central figure in the preservation of Keyblade knowledge and the continuation of the Keyblade legacy.
