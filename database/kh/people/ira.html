PROFILE

Ira was one of the five Foretellers appointed by the Master of Masters during the period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

He was entrusted with leadership of the UNICORNIS Union and was designated as the leader of the Foretellers following the disappearance of the Master of Masters.

Ira's actions during the final period before the Keyblade War played a central role in the conflict between the Foretellers.

THE MASTER OF MASTERS

Ira was directly instructed by the MASTER OF MASTERS and received one of the copies of the Book of Prophecies.

Following the Master's disappearance, Ira assumed responsibility for maintaining order among the Foretellers.
