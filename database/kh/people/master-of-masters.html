PROFILE

The individual designated as the MASTER OF MASTERS is one of the most influential figures in the recorded history of the Keyblade.

He operated during the final period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR and established the system through which the Foretellers and their Unions were organized.

His true identity, origins and ultimate objectives remain unknown.

Historical records indicate that he possessed extraordinary knowledge concerning future events and used that knowledge to prepare for the destruction of the original world.

KEYBLADE MASTERY

The Master of Masters was an exceptionally powerful Keyblade wielder and teacher.

He trained several apprentices whose actions would determine the course of Keyblade history.

His teachings established the foundations of the organizational structure that existed immediately before the Keyblade War.

BOOK OF PROPHECIES
BOOK OF PROPHECIES

The Master of Masters possessed the ability to perceive events beyond his own time.

Using this knowledge, he created the Book of Prophecies and distributed copies among selected apprentices.

The Book became one of the principal instruments through which his plans continued after his disappearance.

THE FORETELLERS
IRA INVI ACED GULA AVA

The Master of Masters selected five Keyblade wielders to become the Foretellers.

Each was assigned a Union and received a copy of the Book of Prophecies.

Their instructions ultimately placed them in opposition to one another and contributed to the events preceding the Keyblade War.

LUXU
LUXU

Luxu was the Master's most secretive apprentice.

He was entrusted with the Keyblade known as NO NAME and given a mission that would continue long after the disappearance of the Master.

Unlike the Foretellers, Luxu was not instructed to participate directly in the coming conflict. His mission required him to observe the passage of history and preserve the Master's plan.

NO NAME
NO NAME

The Master of Masters possessed the Keyblade known as No Name.

The weapon contains the Gazing Eye, allowing its wielder to observe events across time.

The Eye was subsequently entrusted to Luxu as part of the Master's long-term plan.

UNION SYSTEM
THE FORMATION OF THE UNIONS

The Master of Masters established the structure of the five Unions and assigned each to one of his Foretellers.

The system became the dominant organizational structure of Keyblade wielders during the final period before the Keyblade War.

THE DANDELIONS
THE DANDELIONS

The Master of Masters secretly instructed Ava to create a group of Keyblade wielders capable of surviving the coming Keyblade War.

These survivors became known as the Dandelions.

THE TRAITOR

The Master of Masters informed the Foretellers that one of them would betray the others.

The warning was deliberately presented without revealing the identity of the supposed traitor.

This uncertainty became one of the principal causes of the conflict between the Foretellers.

DISAPPEARANCE

The Master of Masters disappeared shortly before the events that led to the Keyblade War.

Before disappearing, he provided his final instructions to his apprentices and entrusted Luxu with his separate mission.

No confirmed record subsequently established his whereabouts for a prolonged period.

THE KEYBLADE WAR
THE KEYBLADE WAR

The Master of Masters was absent from the recorded battlefield during the Keyblade War itself.

His preparations, instructions and manipulation of the Foretellers nevertheless directly shaped the circumstances that culminated in the destruction of the original world.

AFTER THE KEYBLADE WAR
AFTER THE KEYBLADE WAR

The Master's disappearance did not terminate the plan established before the Keyblade War.

Luxu continued the mission entrusted to him, preserving the Master's influence throughout subsequent generations.

LATER HISTORY

For an extended period, no confirmed record established the Master's whereabouts.

His plans nevertheless continued through Luxu and the succession of identities used to observe the development of the Keyblade legacy.

The Master's existence remained relevant to the events leading toward the SECOND KEYBLADE WAR .

QUADRATUM
QUADRATUM

Recent recorded material associated with QUADRATUM provides the latest known sighting of the Master of Masters.

The appearance establishes that the Master is connected to events occurring within this unidentified reality.

His exact activities, objectives and relationship to the events occurring in Quadratum remain unresolved.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The Master of Masters is one of the most influential figures in recorded Keyblade history.

The organizational structure, knowledge and instructions he established directly shaped the events surrounding the Keyblade War and the survival of the Keyblade legacy.

Through Luxu, his influence extended across generations and remained connected to the events leading toward the Second Keyblade War.

His recent appearance in connection with Quadratum establishes a direct link between the ancient Keyblade era and the current unresolved events surrounding Sora.
