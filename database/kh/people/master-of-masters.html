PROFILE

The individual designated as the MASTER OF MASTERS is the earliest known architect of the system that governed the Keyblade wielders during the final period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

His true identity, origins and ultimate objectives remain unknown.

Historical records indicate that he possessed extraordinary knowledge concerning the future and the events that would eventually lead to the destruction of the world.

KEYBLADE MASTERY

The Master of Masters was an exceptionally powerful Keyblade wielder and teacher.

He established a system of apprentices and entrusted them with knowledge that would shape the history of the Keyblade wielders for generations.

BOOK OF PROPHECIES
BOOK OF PROPHECIES

The Master of Masters possessed a unique ability that allowed him to perceive events beyond his own time.

Using this knowledge, he created the Book of Prophecies and distributed copies among his chosen apprentices.

THE FORETELLERS
IRA INVI ACED GULA AVA

The Master of Masters selected five Keyblade wielders to become the Foretellers.

Each was assigned a Union and received a copy of the Book of Prophecies.

LUXU
LUXU

Luxu was the Master's most secretive apprentice.

He was entrusted with the Keyblade known as the No Name and given a mission that would continue long after the disappearance of the Master and the destruction of the original world.

NO NAME
NO NAME

The Master of Masters possessed the Keyblade known as No Name.

The weapon contained the Eye that allowed its wielder to observe events across time.

UNION SYSTEM
THE FORMATION OF THE UNIONS

The Master of Masters established the structure of the five Unions and assigned each to one of his Foretellers.

This system became the dominant organizational structure of Keyblade wielders immediately before the Keyblade War.

THE DANDELIONS
THE DANDELIONS

The Master of Masters secretly instructed Ava to create a group of Keyblade wielders capable of surviving the coming Keyblade War.

These survivors became known as the Dandelions.

THE TRAITOR

The Master of Masters informed the Foretellers that one of them would betray the others.

The warning was deliberately presented without revealing the identity of the supposed traitor.

This uncertainty became one of the principal causes of the conflict between the Foretellers.

DISAPPEARANCE

The Master of Masters disappeared shortly before the events that led to the Keyblade War.

Before disappearing, he provided his final instructions to his apprentices and entrusted Luxu with a separate mission.

THE KEYBLADE WAR
THE KEYBLADE WAR

The Master of Masters was absent from the recorded events of the Keyblade War itself.

His preparations and instructions nevertheless directly influenced the events that culminated in the destruction of the original world.

LATER HISTORY

No reliable historical record confirms the Master's whereabouts following his disappearance.

His plans continued through the actions of Luxu and the surviving Keyblade wielders.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The Master of Masters is considered one of the most influential figures in recorded Keyblade history.

The organizational structure, knowledge and instructions he established directly shaped the events surrounding the Keyblade War and the survival of the Keyblade legacy.

His true intentions remain one of the greatest unresolved subjects within the archive.
