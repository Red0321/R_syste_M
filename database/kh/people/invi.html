PROFILE

Invi was one of the five Foretellers appointed by the MASTER OF MASTERS during the period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

She was entrusted with leadership of the ANGUIS Union and was responsible for observing the actions of the other Foretellers.

Her role placed her in a unique position within the group, particularly following the disappearance of the Master of Masters.

UNION ASSOCIATION
ANGUIS

Invi served as the Foreteller responsible for the Anguis Union.

Her Union formed one of the five principal organizations of Keyblade wielders operating before the Keyblade War.

BOOK OF PROPHECIES

Invi received a copy of the Book of Prophecies from the Master of Masters.

The contents of the book became one of the principal sources of information concerning the events that were believed to precede the Keyblade War.

OBSERVER ROLE

Invi was instructed to observe the actions of the other Foretellers and report information concerning their behavior.

This responsibility became increasingly significant after the disappearance of the Master of Masters and the emergence of accusations concerning a traitor among the Foretellers.
