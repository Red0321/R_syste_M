PROFILE

Lauriam was a Keyblade wielder who lived during the period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

He was associated with the Dandelions and became involved in the events surrounding the survival of the Keyblade wielders after the destruction of the original world.

Lauriam is also the original identity of the individual later known as MARLUXIA .

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Lauriam represents a direct connection between the ancient Keyblade era and the later history of Organization XIII.

His transformation into Marluxia resulted in the preservation of an individual whose original identity had been separated from his later existence.

The relationship between Lauriam and Marluxia is therefore considered a confirmed identity continuity within the archive.
