PROFILE

Urd was a young Keyblade wielder and student of Master Odin at SCALA AD CAELUM .

She belonged to the same generation of students as XEHANORT , ERAQUS , Hermod, Bragi and Vor.

Urd was regarded as one of the more disciplined and composed students within the group.

SCALA AD CAELUM
SCALA AD CAELUM

Urd trained as a Keyblade wielder in Scala ad Caelum under Master Odin.

Her training brought her into regular contact with the other students and eventually placed her directly within the investigation into the disappearances occurring in the city.

XEHANORT

She participated alongside him in the investigation surrounding the missing Keyblade wielders.

DARK ROAD

Urd became involved in the investigation into the disappearance of several upper-class Keyblade wielders.

The investigation eventually revealed the existence of a larger threat connected to Darkness.

UNDERWORLD INVESTIGATION
UNDERWORLD

Urd participated in the investigation into the deaths of the missing Keyblade wielders.

The group travelled to the Underworld in an attempt to obtain information from the spirits of the deceased.

BALDR
BALDR

Urd was connected to the investigation surrounding Baldr and the Darkness that had begun influencing him.

The deaths of the students eventually exposed Baldr's role in the crisis affecting Scala ad Caelum.

BALDR CONFLICT
BALDR CONFLICT

Urd became directly involved in the conflict surrounding Baldr and the Darkness controlling him.

The conflict ultimately resulted in her death.

FINAL FATE

Urd was killed during the events surrounding the Baldr Conflict in Scala ad Caelum.

Her death became one of the losses that defined Xehanort and Eraqus's experience during their youth.

AFTERLIFE

Urd's spirit remained associated with the Underworld following her death.

The spirits of the deceased Keyblade wielders eventually became part of the revelations surrounding the Darkness responsible for the incidents.

Urd was one of the Keyblade wielders whose death contributed to the investigation into the Darkness affecting Scala ad Caelum.

Her death became part of the chain of events that ultimately exposed Baldr's connection to the Darkness.

The loss of Urd and the other students became one of the formative experiences of Xehanort and Eraqus's youth.
