PROFILE

Vor was a young Keyblade wielder and student of Master Odin at SCALA AD CAELUM .

She belonged to the same generation of Keyblade wielders as XEHANORT , ERAQUS , Urd, Hermod and Bragi.

Vor was an active member of the group of students involved in the investigation surrounding the disappearances in Scala ad Caelum.

SCALA AD CAELUM

Vor trained as a Keyblade wielder in Scala ad Caelum under Master Odin.

Her training took place during the period in which Xehanort and Eraqus were investigating the disappearance of their fellow students.

BALDR
BALDR

Vor became connected to the investigation into Baldr and the Darkness that had begun influencing him.

The eventual revelation of Baldr's role placed Vor directly within the final crisis in Scala ad Caelum.

BALDR CONFLICT
BALDR CONFLICT

Vor participated in the confrontation surrounding Baldr and the Darkness controlling him.

The conflict resulted in the deaths of several Keyblade wielders and brought the investigation to its conclusion.

FINAL FATE

Vor survived the initial stages of the Baldr incident and participated in the final confrontation.

She was ultimately killed during the conflict surrounding Baldr.

AFTERLIFE

Vor's spirit remained connected to the deceased Keyblade wielders following her death.

The spirits of the fallen students became part of the revelations surrounding the Darkness that had affected Scala ad Caelum.
