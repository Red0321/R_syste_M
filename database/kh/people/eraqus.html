PROFILE

Eraqus was a Keyblade wielder who became one of the most influential Keyblade Masters of the generation preceding the SECOND KEYBLADE WAR .

He was a close friend and later ideological opponent of XEHANORT .

Eraqus eventually became Master of the Land of Departure and trained a new generation of Keyblade wielders, including Terra, Aqua and Ventus.

DARK ROAD PERIOD

Eraqus was a student at Scala ad Caelum during the period in which Xehanort received his early Keyblade training.

The two became close friends and participated together in the investigations surrounding the disappearances and deaths of their fellow students.

The events surrounding Baldr became a defining experience for both of them and contributed to their increasingly different views concerning darkness.

XEHANORT
XEHANORT

Xehanort was Eraqus's closest friend during their years of training.

Although they shared many experiences, their philosophies eventually diverged.

Eraqus believed that darkness represented a threat that had to be contained, while Xehanort sought to understand its nature and its role in the balance of the worlds.

KEYBLADE MASTERY

Eraqus eventually became a Keyblade Master and continued his training under the established traditions of the Keyblade wielders.

His mastery and philosophy eventually led him to become one of the principal guardians of the light.

LAND OF DEPARTURE
LAND OF DEPARTURE

Eraqus became the Master of the Land of Departure.

From there he trained successive generations of Keyblade wielders and maintained the traditions associated with the protection of the light.

TERRA
TERRA

Terra was one of Eraqus's apprentices.

Eraqus recognized Terra's strength but remained concerned about his susceptibility to darkness.

Their relationship became increasingly strained as Terra's connection to Xehanort deepened.

AQUA
AQUA

Aqua was one of Eraqus's apprentices and ultimately became a Keyblade Master.

Eraqus placed considerable trust in Aqua and regarded her as one of his most accomplished students.

VENTUS
VENTUS

Ventus became Eraqus's apprentice after being brought to the Land of Departure.

Eraqus attempted to protect Ventus from the influence of darkness and trained him alongside Terra and Aqua.

XEHANORT'S INFLUENCE

Eraqus became increasingly concerned with Xehanort's experiments and interest in darkness.

Their disagreement eventually developed into a direct conflict over the fate of Terra, Ventus and the balance between light and darkness.

BIRTH BY SLEEP
BIRTH BY SLEEP

Eraqus became directly involved in the events surrounding Terra, Aqua, Ventus and Xehanort.

His attempts to prevent Ventus from being used in Xehanort's plans ultimately led to a confrontation between Eraqus and Terra.

TERRA'S FALL

Eraqus attempted to eliminate Ventus's connection to darkness after learning of Xehanort's plans.

Terra opposed him in order to protect Ventus.

Xehanort subsequently struck Eraqus and transferred his heart into Terra's body.

FINAL FATE

Eraqus's heart survived within Terra after his defeat.

His presence later became essential to Terra's restoration during the events following the Second Keyblade War.

SECOND KEYBLADE WAR
SECOND KEYBLADE WAR

Eraqus's heart remained connected to Terra during the conflict surrounding the Second Keyblade War.

At the final confrontation with Xehanort, Eraqus emerged from within Terra and helped restore Terra's original heart.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Eraqus was one of the principal guardians of the light during the period preceding the Second Keyblade War.

His friendship with Xehanort and subsequent ideological conflict became one of the central relationships leading to the Second Keyblade War.

Through Terra, Aqua and Ventus, Eraqus's influence extended into the next generation of Keyblade wielders.

CURRENT STATUS

Eraqus is deceased.

His heart was released from Terra following the defeat of Xehanort and his presence was last recorded during the restoration of Terra.
