PROFILE

Ava was one of the five Foretellers appointed by the MASTER OF MASTERS during the period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

She was entrusted with leadership of the VULPES Union.

Unlike the other Foretellers, Ava was given a separate mission by the Master of Masters: selecting a group of Keyblade wielders who would survive the coming destruction.

UNION ASSOCIATION
VULPES

Ava served as the Foreteller responsible for the Vulpes Union.

Her responsibilities eventually extended beyond the ordinary Union structure as she prepared for the coming Keyblade War.

THE MASTER OF MASTERS

Ava received direct instructions from the MASTER OF MASTERS concerning the future of the Keyblade wielders.

She was entrusted with a secret mission that differed from the instructions given to the other Foretellers.

THE DANDELIONS
THE DANDELIONS

Ava was instructed to select Keyblade wielders who would survive the Keyblade War.

These selected wielders became known as the Dandelions.

The Dandelions were intended to preserve the light and continue the Keyblade legacy after the destruction of the original world.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Ava's actions were fundamental to the survival of a portion of the Keyblade wielders after the Keyblade War.

The Dandelions became the foundation through which the Keyblade legacy continued into the following historical period.

Her mission therefore represents one of the most important links between the ancient Keyblade era and the history that followed it.

CURRENT STATUS
