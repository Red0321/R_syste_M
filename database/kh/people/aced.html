PROFILE

Aced was one of the five Foretellers appointed by the MASTER OF MASTERS during the period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

He was entrusted with leadership of the URSUS Union.

Aced's distrust of Ira and his interpretation of the Master's instructions became a major factor in the conflict between the Foretellers.
