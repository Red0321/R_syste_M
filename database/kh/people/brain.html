PROFILE

Brain was a Keyblade wielder active during the final period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

He was associated with DAYBREAK TOWN and became one of the principal figures involved in the events surrounding the Dandelions.

Brain was distinguished by his analytical abilities and his willingness to question the information provided by the Foretellers.
