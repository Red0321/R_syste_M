PROFILE

Hermod was a young Keyblade wielder and student of Master Odin at SCALA AD CAELUM .

He belonged to the same generation of students as XEHANORT , ERAQUS , Urd, Vor and Bragi.

Hermod became involved in the investigation into the disappearance of several upper-class Keyblade wielders.

FINAL FATE

Hermod was killed during the Baldr Conflict in Scala ad Caelum.

His death became one of the losses suffered by Xehanort and Eraqus during their youth.

The spirits of the fallen students became important to the investigation surrounding Darkness and Baldr.
