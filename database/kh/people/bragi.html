IDENTITY

Bragi was the identity used by LUXU during the period surrounding Xehanort's youth in Scala ad Caelum.

Under the appearance and identity of Bragi, Luxu was able to operate among the Keyblade wielders of Scala ad Caelum without revealing his true identity.

The Bragi identity is therefore recorded as a distinct identity while remaining chronologically connected to Luxu.

SCALA AD CAELUM
SCALA AD CAELUM

Bragi operated within Scala ad Caelum during the period in which Xehanort and Eraqus were training as Keyblade wielders.

His position within the student population allowed Luxu to observe the events taking place within the city from close range.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The Bragi identity represents one of the known stages in Luxu's long-term mission.

Its importance lies primarily in Luxu's proximity to Xehanort during the formative period in which his views concerning darkness and the world were developing.

The identity also demonstrates the extent to which Luxu was able to remain embedded within successive generations of Keyblade wielders without revealing his true identity.
