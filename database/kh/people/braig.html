IDENTITY

Braig was the identity used by LUXU during the period surrounding the events of Birth by Sleep.

Under the identity of Braig, Luxu established a close relationship with XEHANORT and became involved in the events surrounding Terra, Aqua and Ventus.

The Braig identity is maintained as an independent record because Braig participated in the events of this period as a distinct public identity.

RADIANT GARDEN
RADIANT GARDEN

Braig was associated with Radiant Garden during the period in which Xehanort became involved with Ansem the Wise's research.

His position allowed him to remain close to Xehanort while concealing the larger purpose behind his actions.

XEHANORT
XEHANORT

Xehanort was the most important individual associated with Braig during this period.

Braig deliberately positioned himself close to Xehanort and assisted him in the events leading toward the creation of Terra-Xehanort.

Xehanort eventually regarded Braig as a useful participant in his plans.

TERRA
TERRA

Braig deliberately provoked Terra during the events surrounding the Mark of Mastery.

His actions contributed to Terra's increasing connection with darkness and attracted Xehanort's attention toward him as a potential vessel.

BIRTH BY SLEEP
BIRTH BY SLEEP

Braig played an active role in Xehanort's plans during the events of Birth by Sleep.

He confronted Terra and deliberately manipulated circumstances in order to encourage Terra's use of darkness.

Braig was eventually defeated by Terra, but survived the encounter.

BRAIG AND TERRA

Braig intentionally challenged Terra's emotional control during their confrontation.

His actions were designed to make Terra draw upon the darkness within his heart.

The encounter demonstrated Braig's role as an active manipulator rather than a simple ally of Xehanort.

XEHANORT'S PLAN

Braig assisted Xehanort in carrying out the plan to create a suitable vessel.

His actions helped place Terra in circumstances that made him increasingly vulnerable to Xehanort's influence.

TERRA-XEHANORT
TERRA-XEHANORT

Following the transfer of Xehanort's heart into Terra, the resulting individual became Terra-Xehanort.

Braig remained connected to Xehanort's plans following this transformation.

XEHANORT'S HEART

Braig's involvement with Xehanort was not merely based on loyalty.

The identity was part of Luxu's continued observation of Xehanort and the development of the events that would eventually lead to the Second Keyblade War.

ORGANIZATION XIII
ORGANIZATION XIII

Braig later became connected to Organization XIII through his transformation into a Nobody.

The Nobody associated with this identity became known as Xigbar.

XIGBAR
XIGBAR

Xigbar is the Nobody associated with the Braig identity.

The historical record additionally establishes that Luxu was operating through the Braig and later Xigbar identities.

Xigbar is therefore maintained as a separate record rather than being treated as a simple duplicate of Braig.
