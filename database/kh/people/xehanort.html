PROFILE

Xehanort was a Keyblade wielder whose life and actions became central to the events leading to the Second Keyblade War.

Born on the DESTINY ISLANDS , Xehanort possessed an unusually strong curiosity concerning the worlds beyond his home.

His desire to understand the nature of the world, light, darkness and the human heart eventually led him to become one of the most powerful and dangerous Keyblade Masters in recorded history.

DESTINY ISLANDS
DESTINY ISLANDS

Xehanort spent his early childhood on the Destiny Islands.

His isolation and repeated visions of worlds beyond the islands contributed to his desire to leave and seek knowledge elsewhere.

SCALA AD CAELUM
SCALA AD CAELUM

Xehanort travelled to Scala ad Caelum after receiving an invitation to become a Keyblade wielder.

There he began his formal training and met the individuals who would define his early life.

DARK ROAD
XEHANORT'S ARRIVAL AT SCALA DARK ROAD INCIDENTS BALDR CONFLICT DARK ROAD FINAL BATTLE

During his youth in Scala ad Caelum, Xehanort participated in a series of investigations involving missing and deceased Keyblade wielders.

The events surrounding Baldr became a decisive influence on his developing understanding of darkness.

ERAQUS
ERAQUS

Eraqus was Xehanort's closest friend during their years of training.

Their friendship survived their early investigations and eventual advancement as Keyblade wielders.

Their opposing philosophies concerning light and darkness eventually transformed their friendship into ideological conflict.

BALDR
BALDR

Xehanort encountered Baldr during his years of training in Scala ad Caelum.

The Baldr Conflict became one of the most important formative experiences of Xehanort's youth.

Baldr's descent into darkness reinforced Xehanort's interest in understanding the nature of darkness rather than simply rejecting it.

KEYBLADE MASTERY

Xehanort eventually became a Keyblade Master.

His growing knowledge of the history of the Keyblade and the nature of the worlds led him to investigate subjects considered dangerous by other Masters.

MARK OF MASTERY

Xehanort and Eraqus eventually advanced through the Keyblade training system and became Masters.

Their success established them as members of the generation that would later shape the events preceding the Second Keyblade War.

ANCIENT KEYBLADE HISTORY

Xehanort became increasingly interested in the ancient history of the Keyblade and the original Keyblade War.

He sought to understand why the world had been divided and why darkness continued to exist despite the efforts of Keyblade wielders.

BRAIG
BRAIG LUXU

Xehanort encountered Braig during the period surrounding the events of Birth by Sleep.

Braig became involved in Xehanort's plans and ultimately became one of the vessels through which Xehanort's influence was maintained.

Historical records subsequently established that Braig was an identity used by Luxu.

TERRA
TERRA

Xehanort identified Terra as a suitable vessel for his plans because of Terra's strength and susceptibility to darkness.

Xehanort manipulated Terra during the events of Birth by Sleep and eventually transferred his heart into Terra's body.

BIRTH BY SLEEP
BIRTH BY SLEEP

Xehanort manipulated the events surrounding Terra, Aqua and Ventus in preparation for the creation of a new vessel and the recreation of the conditions of the original Keyblade War.

His actions culminated in the confrontation at the Keyblade Graveyard.

TERRA-XEHANORT
TERRA-XEHANORT

Xehanort successfully transferred his heart into Terra's body.

The resulting individual became known as Terra-Xehanort.

LOSS OF MEMORY

Following the events involving Terra-Xehanort, Xehanort lost his memories.

He subsequently came to be associated with the research carried out in Radiant Garden.

ANSEM THE WISE
ANSEM THE WISE

Xehanort became an apprentice to Ansem the Wise in Radiant Garden.

His experiments concerning the heart and darkness eventually led to the downfall of the original research group.

ANSEM, SEEKER OF DARKNESS
ANSEM, SEEKER OF DARKNESS

Xehanort's Heartless became known as Ansem, Seeker of Darkness.

This form represented the separation of Xehanort's heart from his original body.

XEMNAS
XEMNAS

Xehanort's Nobody became known as Xemnas.

Xemnas became the founder and leader of Organization XIII.

ORGANIZATION XIII
ORGANIZATION XIII

Xehanort established Organization XIII through Xemnas and used the organization to gather vessels and manipulate the development of the Keyblade War.

The organization became the principal instrument through which Xehanort's long-term plan was executed.

VESSELS

Xehanort sought thirteen vessels capable of containing fragments of his heart.

The vessels were intended to oppose the seven lights and recreate the conditions required for the χ-blade and Kingdom Hearts.

SORA
SORA

Sora repeatedly opposed Xehanort's plans across multiple conflicts.

Xehanort eventually identified Sora as one of the principal obstacles to the completion of his objective.

SECOND KEYBLADE WAR
SECOND KEYBLADE WAR

Xehanort deliberately recreated the conditions of the ancient Keyblade War in order to summon Kingdom Hearts and obtain the χ-blade.

His thirteen vessels confronted the seven guardians of light at the Keyblade Graveyard.

The resulting conflict became known as the Second Keyblade War.

KINGDOM HEARTS

Xehanort's ultimate objective was to obtain Kingdom Hearts and use its power to reset the world.

He believed that the division between light and darkness had created an unstable world and intended to create a new order through the power of Kingdom Hearts.

FINAL CONFRONTATION

Xehanort was ultimately defeated by Sora and the Guardians of Light.

The defeat of his various incarnations and vessels brought the Second Keyblade War to an end.

Xehanort's heart was finally released, ending his direct control over the events surrounding the conflict.

ERAQUS

During the final confrontation, Eraqus's heart emerged from within Terra.

Eraqus confronted Xehanort one final time before returning to the hearts of his former companions.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Xehanort is one of the most influential individuals in recorded Keyblade history.

His actions connected the ancient history of the Keyblade War with the modern conflicts involving Sora, the Guardians of Light and Organization XIII.

His attempts to recreate the conditions of the original Keyblade War ultimately resulted in the Second Keyblade War.
