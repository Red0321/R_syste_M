PROFILE

Riku is an individual originating from DESTINY ISLANDS whose recorded activities span multiple conflicts involving the separation and restoration of worlds.

Riku was closely associated with SORA and KAIRI prior to the destruction of Destiny Islands.

His subsequent activities resulted in repeated encounters with the Heartless, Organization XIII and other entities connected to the phenomenon surrounding Kingdom Hearts.
