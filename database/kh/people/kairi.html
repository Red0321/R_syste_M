PROFILE

Kairi is an individual whose life became directly connected to several major incidents involving the separation and restoration of multiple worlds.

Although originally associated with RADIANT GARDEN , Kairi spent a significant portion of her early life on DESTINY ISLANDS , where she became closely associated with SORA and RIKU .

Her existence and connection to the heart became relevant to multiple events involving the Keyblade, the Heartless and the restoration of the worlds.

Kairi was later documented wielding a Keyblade and received formal training in its use. Her involvement in the Second Keyblade War confirmed her status as an active participant in the conflict.

CURRENT STATUS

Kairi remains associated with the Keyblade wielders and the continued protection of the worlds.

Her current activities remain connected to SORA , RIKU and the continuing investigation into events following the Second Keyblade War.
