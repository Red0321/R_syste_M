TRUE IDENTITY

Luxu is one of the six apprentices of the MASTER OF MASTERS .

He was entrusted with a mission intended to continue long after the original Keyblade War and preserve the Master's plan across successive generations.

Unlike the identities he later assumed, Luxu represents the continuous individual behind the recorded identities associated with his mission.

MASTER OF MASTERS
MASTER OF MASTERS

The Master of Masters entrusted Luxu with a long-term mission following the events leading to the Keyblade War.

Luxu was instructed to observe the passage of history and ensure that the Master's plan continued across generations.

His mission included the observation of the Keyblade wielders who would eventually become connected to the events surrounding Xehanort.

NO NAME
NO NAME

Luxu was entrusted with the Keyblade known as No Name.

The weapon was essential to the continuation of the Master's plan and was passed through the generations together with Luxu's mission.

OBSERVATION

Luxu's principal function was to observe rather than directly alter the course of history.

He remained hidden while civilizations changed, Keyblade wielders were born and the events surrounding the ancient conflict gradually became forgotten.

His long-term observation eventually brought him into contact with the generation that included Xehanort and Eraqus.

IDENTITY HISTORY

Luxu repeatedly changed his outward identity in order to continue his mission without revealing his true nature.

PURPOSE OBSERVATION

Luxu assumed the identity of Bragi while operating among the Keyblade wielders of Scala ad Caelum.

Under this identity, he observed Xehanort, Eraqus and the other students during the events surrounding the disappearances in Scala ad Caelum.

The Bragi identity allowed Luxu to remain close to Xehanort while concealing his true identity.

XEHANORT OBSERVATION
XEHANORT

Xehanort became one of the principal individuals observed by Luxu.

Luxu was able to witness Xehanort's development from a young Keyblade wielder into a Master whose actions would eventually reshape the worlds.

The observation of Xehanort became one of the most significant stages of Luxu's long-term mission.

His position within the organization allowed him to continue observing Xehanort's plans from within the structure that had been created to gather vessels for the coming conflict.

XEHANORT
OPEN XEHANORT RECORD

Xehanort became one of the most important individuals in Luxu's recorded mission.

Luxu observed Xehanort across multiple stages of his life and continued to follow the development of his plans through successive identities.

The relationship between Luxu and Xehanort therefore extends across several distinct identities and historical periods.

ERAQUS
ERAQUS

Eraqus was another important figure observed by Luxu during the period of Xehanort's development.

His ideological conflict with Xehanort eventually became one of the defining elements of the historical sequence Luxu was observing.

THE MASTER'S PLAN

Luxu's actions were ultimately connected to the long-term plan established by the Master of Masters.

His mission required patience, concealment and continued observation across generations.

The identities adopted by Luxu allowed him to remain embedded within different periods of history without exposing the continuity behind them.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Luxu represents one of the longest continuous presences in the recorded history of the Keyblade.

His mission connects the age of the original Keyblade War with the events surrounding Xehanort, Organization XIII and the Second Keyblade War.

His successive identities allowed him to remain hidden while continuing to observe the development of the world and the individuals involved in the Master's plan.
