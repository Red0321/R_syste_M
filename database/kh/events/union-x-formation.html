EVENT SUMMARY

Following the instructions of the Master of Masters, the Foretellers established separate groups of Keyblade wielders known as Unions.

The Unions became the principal organizational structure for Keyblade wielders during the period preceding the KEYBLADE WAR .

Each Union operated under the leadership of one of the Foretellers and developed its own identity while continuing to follow the teachings of the Master of Masters.

DAYBREAK TOWN

The formation and activities of the Unions were centered around DAYBREAK TOWN .

The town became the principal known gathering place for the Keyblade wielders associated with the Unions.

PURPOSE

The Unions were established as part of the organizational structure created by the Master of Masters to prepare Keyblade wielders for the events that would follow.

Their members were instructed to collect light and continue the teachings of their respective leaders.

The true purpose behind the system remained unknown to most of its members.

RELATED GROUP
THE DANDELIONS

A selected group of Keyblade wielders known as the Dandelions was established separately from the primary Union structure.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The formation of the Unions established the organizational structure that dominated Keyblade society immediately before the Keyblade War.

The division of the wielders into separate groups would later become a significant factor in the events that preceded the destruction of the world.
