EVENT SUMMARY

Following the conclusion of the SECOND KEYBLADE WAR , Sora disappeared from the known worlds.

Subsequent records indicate that Sora became present within an unidentified reality later associated with QUADRATUM .

The circumstances surrounding Sora's arrival, the nature of the reality and the mechanism responsible for his displacement remain unresolved.

INITIAL OBSERVATION

Sora was observed within an urban environment fundamentally different from the worlds previously documented in this archive.

The environment contained modern infrastructure, large buildings, roadways and other structures inconsistent with the established characteristics of known worlds.

The location was subsequently identified as Quadratum.

YOZORA ENCOUNTER

During the incident, Sora encountered an unidentified individual known as YOZORA .

Available records indicate that Yozora possesses abilities and knowledge that cannot currently be explained through the established understanding of the worlds.

The precise relationship between Yozora and Quadratum remains unknown.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The Quadratum Incident represents the first confirmed indication that individuals associated with the known worlds may become present within a reality outside the previously established world network.

The incident therefore represents a major unresolved development in the current historical record.

CURRENT STATUS

The incident remains unresolved.

Sora's current condition and location remain unknown.

Further information concerning Quadratum, Yozora and the circumstances surrounding Sora's disappearance is required before the incident can be fully reconstructed.
