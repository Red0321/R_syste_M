SUMMARY

A series of incidents occurred during the period in which the young Xehanort trained as a Keyblade wielder in Scala ad Caelum.

The incidents involved the disappearance of Keyblade wielders, the investigation of darkness, and the discovery of information concerning the history of the worlds.

These events significantly influenced the development of Xehanort's beliefs and his later pursuit of knowledge concerning darkness, hearts and the Keyblade.

Xehanort and Eraqus investigated the circumstances surrounding the disappearance of their fellow wielders.

Their investigation gradually revealed that the incidents were connected to a larger threat operating within Scala ad Caelum.

DARKNESS

The incidents forced the young Keyblade wielders to confront the existence and influence of darkness.

Xehanort became increasingly interested in the nature of darkness and the reasons behind its existence.

These experiences would later influence his philosophy concerning light and darkness.

SCALA AD CAELUM

Scala ad Caelum was the primary location affected by the incidents.

The events exposed the vulnerability of the Keyblade training system and forced its students to confront threats beyond their normal instruction.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The incidents of this period represent a fundamental stage in the development of Xehanort's worldview.

His exposure to darkness, loss and the history of the Keyblade shaped the ideas that would later influence his actions as a Master.

The events also established the relationship between Xehanort and Eraqus that would eventually become central to the Second Keyblade War.
