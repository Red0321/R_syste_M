SUMMARY

The Baldr Conflict was a series of events surrounding the descent of the Keyblade wielder Baldr into darkness and the subsequent attack on Scala ad Caelum.

Following the disappearance and deaths of several Keyblade wielders, Baldr was revealed to have been consumed by an incarnation of Darkness.

His objective was to use the deaths of thirteen lights to summon Kingdom Hearts and recreate the world in darkness.

BALDR
BALDR

Baldr was a Keyblade wielder and student of Master Odin in Scala ad Caelum.

He possessed the ability to perceive the contents of other people's hearts, allowing him to sense darkness within them.

Following the death of his sister Hoder, Baldr's grief and fear allowed an incarnation of Darkness to take control of his heart.

HODER
HODER

Hoder was Baldr's older sister and one of the upper-class Keyblade wielders who disappeared during their Mark of Mastery training.

She was killed while attempting to protect Baldr, becoming the event that accelerated his descent into darkness.

THE DARKNESS

The Darkness responsible for Baldr's corruption was an incarnation of True Darkness.

It exploited Baldr's grief and his sensitivity to the darkness within the hearts of others.

The Darkness eventually gained control of Baldr's body and used him to manipulate the surviving Keyblade wielders.

THE MISSING STUDENTS
HODER HEIMDALL HELGI SIGRUN VIDAR VALA VALI

The disappearance of the upper-class students triggered the investigation that eventually exposed Baldr's actions.

THE UNDERWORLD
UNDERWORLD

Xehanort, Eraqus and the other students travelled to the Underworld in an attempt to communicate with the deceased Keyblade wielders.

The investigation revealed the circumstances surrounding the deaths of Hoder, Heimdall, Helgi and Sigrun.

ATTACK ON SCALA AD CAELUM

Following the return of the surviving students, Scala ad Caelum came under attack.

Baldr appeared surrounded by darkness and revealed himself as the individual responsible for the deaths of the missing students.

His objective was to complete the thirteen sacrifices required to summon Kingdom Hearts.

THE LOWER CLASSMEN
XEHANORT ERAQUS URD HERMOD BRAGI VOR

The lower-class students became directly involved in the confrontation with Baldr.

Urd, Hermod and Bragi were killed during the conflict, while Xehanort, Eraqus and Vor became the final targets of Baldr's plan.

THE UPPER CLASSMEN
VIDAR VALA VALI

The surviving upper-class students attempted to oppose Baldr after abandoning their own plans to summon Kingdom Hearts.

Baldr ultimately killed them using the Heartless manifestation of his Darkness.

HRINGHORN

Baldr manifested a massive Heartless known as Hringhorn during the final stage of the conflict.

The creature represented the overwhelming power of the Darkness controlling Baldr.

Xehanort and Eraqus fought Hringhorn together and eventually defeated it.

KINGDOM HEARTS

Baldr intended to use thirteen lights as sacrifices to create thirteen manifestations of Darkness and summon Kingdom Hearts.

His objective was to plunge the world into darkness and use Kingdom Hearts to recreate it.

The plan failed before Kingdom Hearts could be successfully summoned.

FINAL CONFRONTATION

Xehanort and Eraqus confronted Baldr at the top of Scala ad Caelum after defeating Hringhorn.

Eraqus attempted to convince Xehanort to purge the Darkness from Baldr rather than kill him.

Xehanort ultimately chose to kill Baldr, destroying the Darkness controlling him.

HODER'S SPIRIT
HODER

Hoder's spirit appeared during the final confrontation and attempted to reach Baldr.

Her intervention helped expose the emotional connection between Baldr, his Darkness and the people whose hearts he had perceived.

ODIN
ODIN

Master Odin intervened during the final battle and restrained Baldr's Darkness using No Name.

Following Baldr's defeat, Odin decided that Xehanort and Eraqus would become his successors.

AFTERMATH

The deaths of Baldr and the other students left Scala ad Caelum deeply affected.

Xehanort and Eraqus mourned their fallen classmates and later visited their graves.

Odin subsequently prepared to retire and designated Xehanort and Eraqus as his successors.

BRAGI
BRAGI LUXU

Bragi's apparent death during the conflict was later revealed to have been staged.

The individual known as Bragi was secretly possessed by Luxu, who subsequently continued observing Xehanort.

HISTORICAL CONSEQUENCES

The Baldr Conflict became a defining event in the development of Xehanort's understanding of darkness.

Baldr's final words encouraged Xehanort to seek answers concerning the nature of darkness and the contradictions within the hearts of individuals.

These ideas would later contribute to Xehanort's philosophy and ultimately influence the events leading to the Second Keyblade War.
