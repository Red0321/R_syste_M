EVENT SUMMARY

Following the appearance of Nobodies possessing exceptional strength and individual consciousness, a secret organization was established to gather individuals of similar nature under a single command.

The organization eventually became known as ORGANIZATION XIII .

The organization operated from concealed headquarters and gradually expanded its influence across multiple worlds.

ORIGIN

The earliest known members were individuals who had lost their hearts and subsequently continued to exist as Nobodies.

Among the founders was XEMNAS , the Nobody of ANSEM .

The organization's activities were closely connected to research concerning the heart, darkness and the existence of Nobodies.

ORGANIZATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

As the organization expanded, additional Nobodies were recruited and assigned numerical designations according to their position within the group.

The organization established operational divisions across several worlds and began conducting activities intended to advance its understanding of hearts and Kingdom Hearts.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The formation of Organization XIII marked the beginning of one of the most significant recorded conspiracies involving the worlds.

Its subsequent activities would eventually bring the organization into direct conflict with multiple Keyblade wielders and contribute to several major incidents recorded in this archive.
