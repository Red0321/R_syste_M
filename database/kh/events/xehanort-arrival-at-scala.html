SUMMARY

Following the events of the original Keyblade War, the young Xehanort left his home on Destiny Islands and travelled to Scala ad Caelum after receiving an invitation to become a Keyblade wielder.

His arrival marked the beginning of his formal training and established the period in which he would come into contact with other young Keyblade wielders.

SCALA AD CAELUM
SCALA AD CAELUM

Scala ad Caelum served as the principal training ground and home of the Keyblade wielders during this period.

Xehanort's arrival brought him into an environment fundamentally different from his isolated life on the Destiny Islands.
