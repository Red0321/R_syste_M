OVERVIEW

The period designated as AFTER THE KEYBLADE WAR encompasses the aftermath of the destruction of the original world and the beginning of a new era for the surviving Keyblade wielders.

The original civilization of Daybreak Town was destroyed during the war. The surviving Dandelions were separated from the world that existed before the conflict and became the foundation of the next generation of Keyblade wielders.

Rather than allowing the survivors to recreate the same divisions that produced the war, the Dandelions established a new structure known as Union χ.

The destruction of Daybreak Town eventually gave rise to Scala ad Caelum, a new center of Keyblade wielding civilization built upon the remains of the former world.

DESTRUCTION OF DAYBREAK TOWN
DAYBREAK TOWN

The Keyblade War resulted in the destruction of the world in which the Foretellers and their Unions had lived.

Daybreak Town was left in ruins following the conflict.

The destruction of the city marked the end of the ancient civilization and the beginning of the reconstruction of the Keyblade legacy.

THE DANDELIONS
THE DANDELIONS EPHEMER SKULD LAURIAM VENTUS BRAIN PLAYER

The Dandelions were the principal survivors of the Keyblade War.

Their purpose had been to preserve the light and rebuild the world after the destruction foretold by the Master of Masters.

Following the war, the surviving wielders were gathered according to Ava's instructions.

UNION χ

The surviving Dandelions established a new organization in which former members of the five Unions could work together rather than remain divided.

This new structure became known as UNION χ.

Its purpose was to preserve the balance of light and prevent the divisions that had contributed to the original Keyblade War.

MEMORY OF THE WAR

The events of the Keyblade War were deliberately concealed from most of the surviving Dandelions.

Only the selected Union leaders retained direct knowledge of the previous world and the conflict that destroyed it.

The suppression of this knowledge was intended to prevent the survivors from repeating the same conflict.

NEW UNION LEADERS
EPHEMER SKULD VENTUS BRAIN LAURIAM

Five individuals were selected to become the new Union leaders following the destruction of the original world.

Ephemer, Skuld, Ventus, Brain and Lauriam became central figures in the reconstruction of the Keyblade wielding society.

STRELITZIA

Strelitzia had been selected to become one of the new Union leaders.

Her death before the Keyblade War prevented her from assuming that role.

Lauriam's later search for information concerning his sister became one of the major unresolved conflicts among the surviving Dandelions.

PLAYER
PLAYER

The Player survived the events surrounding the Keyblade War and became part of the new generation of Keyblade wielders.

The Player's memories and eventual fate became connected to the unusual circumstances surrounding the post-war world.

DAYBREAK TOWN DATA

Although the original Daybreak Town was destroyed, its legacy continued through the surviving Dandelions.

A reconstructed version of Daybreak Town later existed within a data environment, allowing the surviving wielders to continue their lives while preserving the appearance of their former world.

SCALA AD CAELUM
SCALA AD CAELUM

Scala ad Caelum was established by Ephemer after the destruction of Daybreak Town.

The city was constructed upon the ruins of the former world and eventually became the central civilization of the Keyblade wielders.

Scala ad Caelum would later become the center of Keyblade education and the birthplace of a new generation of Masters.

EPHEMER
EPHEMER

Ephemer became one of the principal figures in the reconstruction of the world following the Keyblade War.

His leadership ultimately resulted in the establishment of Scala ad Caelum.

The city would become the foundation of the civilization from which later generations of Keyblade wielders emerged.

BRAIN
BRAIN

Brain became one of the five new Union leaders following the Keyblade War.

His investigation into the events surrounding the destruction of the old world eventually led him to question the information provided to the new generation.

LAURIAM
LAURIAM

Lauriam became one of the new Union leaders after the Keyblade War.

His continued investigation into the disappearance of Strelitzia would eventually expose connections between the old world and the new one.

VENTUS
VENTUS

Ventus was selected as one of the new Union leaders.

His presence in the new era would eventually connect the ancient history of the Keyblade with later generations of wielders.

DARKNESS

The events following the Keyblade War revealed that the conflict had not simply ended with the destruction of the old world.

The surviving Dandelions remained connected to forces that had existed before the war.

The continued presence of Darkness became one of the principal threats to the new world.

DATA DAYBREAK TOWN

A data reconstruction of Daybreak Town was used to preserve the surviving Dandelions and allow them to continue their existence without the knowledge of the original world's destruction.

This artificial environment became the setting for events that would further divide the surviving wielders and reveal the unresolved consequences of the Keyblade War.

CHIRITHY
CHIRITHY

The Chirithy remained connected to the hearts of the surviving Keyblade wielders.

Following the destruction of the old world, their continued existence became tied to the survival of the Dandelions.

TRANSITION TO SCALA AD CAELUM

As the survivors moved beyond the events of the original Keyblade War, the ruins of Daybreak Town became the foundation for a new civilization.

Scala ad Caelum emerged as the new center of Keyblade wielding society.

Generations later, the city would become the setting in which the history of Xehanort and Eraqus began.

CONNECTION TO DARK ROAD
XEHANORT'S ARRIVAL AT SCALA DARK ROAD INCIDENTS

The civilization established after the Keyblade War eventually became the setting for the events of Dark Road.

The passage from this period into the era of Xehanort represents the continuation of the Keyblade legacy established by the survivors of the ancient war.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The aftermath of the Keyblade War marks the transition between the ancient civilization and the world known to later generations.

The destruction of Daybreak Town ended the old system of Unions and created the conditions for a new Keyblade civilization.

The Dandelions preserved the light and carried the Keyblade legacy into the future.

The establishment of Scala ad Caelum created the foundation from which the later generations of Keyblade Masters would emerge.

This record is classified as CONFIRMED / COMPLETE.

The period is documented through records concerning the Dandelions, Union χ, the destruction of Daybreak Town and the establishment of Scala ad Caelum.
