SUMMARY

The Dark Road Final Battle was the concluding confrontation between Xehanort, Eraqus and the Darkness possessing Baldr.

After the deaths of the Keyblade wielders and the revelation of Baldr's role in the incidents surrounding Scala ad Caelum, the surviving students confronted him during his attempt to complete his plan.

The confrontation resulted in Baldr's defeat and the destruction of the Darkness that had taken control of him.

BALDR
BALDR

Baldr had become completely consumed by the Darkness that had manipulated his grief following the death of his sister Hoder.

He attempted to complete the sacrifices required to summon Kingdom Hearts and recreate the world according to his own vision.

XEHANORT
XEHANORT

Xehanort confronted Baldr alongside Eraqus.

The battle became one of the defining experiences of Xehanort's youth and influenced his later understanding of darkness and the human heart.

ERAQUS
ERAQUS

Eraqus fought alongside Xehanort during the final confrontation.

Unlike Xehanort, Eraqus believed that Baldr could potentially be saved by separating him from the Darkness.

HRINGHORN

The Darkness manifested itself through the massive Heartless known as Hringhorn.

Xehanort and Eraqus fought the creature before confronting Baldr directly.

Hringhorn was ultimately defeated during the battle.

KINGDOM HEARTS

Baldr intended to use the deaths of thirteen lights to create thirteen manifestations of Darkness and summon Kingdom Hearts.

The final confrontation prevented the completion of this plan.

HODER
HODER

Hoder's spirit appeared during the confrontation and attempted to reach her brother.

Her presence reinforced the connection between Baldr's grief and the Darkness that had consumed him.

FINAL CONFRONTATION

After defeating Hringhorn, Xehanort and Eraqus confronted Baldr directly.

Eraqus attempted to convince Xehanort that Baldr's Darkness could be removed without killing him.

Xehanort ultimately chose to eliminate Baldr, destroying the Darkness controlling him.

ODIN
ODIN

Master Odin intervened during the final stage of the conflict.

Following the defeat of Baldr, Odin determined that Xehanort and Eraqus would become his successors.

BRAGI
BRAGI LUXU

Bragi's apparent death during the preceding conflict was later revealed to have been staged.

The individual operating under Bragi's identity was Luxu, who had infiltrated the group in order to continue observing Xehanort.

AFTERMATH

The defeat of Baldr brought the immediate crisis in Scala ad Caelum to an end.

The surviving Keyblade wielders mourned the classmates who had died during the conflict.

Xehanort and Eraqus subsequently continued their training under Master Odin.

XEHANORT'S DEVELOPMENT

The events surrounding Baldr became a major influence on Xehanort's developing philosophy.

Baldr's actions demonstrated to Xehanort that darkness could emerge from grief, fear and the contradictions within a person's heart.

These observations contributed to Xehanort's later pursuit of knowledge concerning darkness, light and the nature of the world.

XEHANORT AND ERAQUS

The final battle reinforced the differences between Xehanort and Eraqus concerning darkness.

Eraqus continued to believe that darkness should be rejected and controlled, while Xehanort became increasingly interested in understanding its nature.

This ideological difference would become increasingly significant as both characters advanced toward the Mark of Mastery.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The final battle against Baldr marked the conclusion of one of the defining incidents of Xehanort's youth.

The experience influenced the beliefs that would later guide Xehanort's actions and contributed to the ideological division between him and Eraqus.

The aftermath also marked the beginning of the final stage of their training before becoming Keyblade Masters.
