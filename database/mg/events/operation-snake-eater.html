COVERT MILITARY OPERATION

The operation was conducted by the United States government through the FOX unit during the Cold War.

Its primary objectives were the recovery of Soviet scientist Nikolai Stepanovich Sokolov, the destruction of the Shagohod and the elimination of The Boss.

BACKGROUND

The operation followed the failure of the VIRTUOUS MISSION , during which Sokolov was recaptured by Soviet forces and The Boss defected to the Soviet Union.

The destruction of the OKB-754 research facility by a nuclear-capable Davy Crockett weapon created an international crisis and placed additional pressure on the United States to resolve the incident.

The United States therefore authorized a second infiltration of Soviet territory.

PRIMARY PARTICIPANTS
NAKED SNAKE THE BOSS EVA MAJOR ZERO PARA-MEDIC SIGINT COLONEL VOLGIN OCELOT NIKOLAI SOKOLOV
OPERATION

Naked Snake was deployed into Tselinoyarsk and instructed to recover Sokolov and eliminate The Boss.

During the operation, Snake encountered EVA, Ocelot and members of the Cobra Unit while attempting to locate Sokolov and reach the Soviet military installation at Groznyj Grad.

Snake subsequently discovered information concerning Aleksandr Granin and the existence of a competing weapons project known as METAL GEAR .

GROZNYJ GRAD

The Groznyj Grad military complex served as one of the principal locations during the operation.

Sokolov was held at the facility, while Colonel Volgin supervised the development of the Shagohod.

Snake ultimately infiltrated the facility and destroyed the Shagohod during the final stage of the operation.

SHAGOHOD

The Shagohod was a Soviet experimental weapon system developed by Sokolov.

The machine represented one of the most significant military technologies involved in the operation.

SHAGOHOD
FINAL CONFRONTATION

The final stage of the operation culminated in a confrontation between Naked Snake and The Boss.

Snake defeated his former mentor and completed the mission ordered by the United States.

Despite the official interpretation of The Boss as a traitor, later information established that her actions had been part of a larger political operation.

OUTCOME

The Shagohod was destroyed, Colonel Volgin was defeated and The Boss was eliminated.

Sokolov was recovered and part of the PHILOSOPHERS' LEGACY was returned to the United States.

The operation successfully prevented the immediate continuation of the Shagohod project.

HISTORICAL CONSEQUENCES

The operation fundamentally altered the life of Naked Snake.

Following the mission, Snake was awarded the title of BIG BOSS .

The events surrounding The Boss, the Philosophers' Legacy and the Metal Gear concept would subsequently influence decades of military and political history.
