THE VIRTUOUS MISSION
EVENT RECORD
DATE 24 AUGUST 1964
LOCATION TSELINOYARSK
STATUS FAILED
CLASSIFICATION

COVERT MILITARY OPERATION

The Virtuous Mission was a United States intelligence operation conducted during the Cold War.

The mission was intended to recover Soviet scientist Nikolai Stepanovich Sokolov, who had been developing a new military weapons system.

BACKGROUND

Soviet weapons scientist NIKOLAI SOKOLOV attempted to defect from the Soviet Union after becoming concerned about the military application of his research.

Sokolov was subsequently located in Tselinoyarsk and placed under the protection of the United States intelligence service.

Soviet pressure eventually forced the United States to authorize a covert operation to extract him from Soviet territory.

PRIMARY PARTICIPANTS
NAKED SNAKE THE BOSS NIKOLAI SOKOLOV MAJOR ZERO PARA-MEDIC SIGINT
DEPLOYMENT

Naked Snake was deployed into Tselinoyarsk to locate Sokolov and escort him to the designated extraction point.

Snake entered Soviet territory alone and established contact with Sokolov near the Rassvet research facility.

TSELINOYARSK RASSVET
SOKOLOV EXTRACTION

Snake successfully located Sokolov and learned that the scientist was being forced to continue his work on a classified weapons project.

Sokolov identified the project as the Shagohod, a Soviet weapon capable of dramatically increasing the strategic range of nuclear missiles.

SHAGOHOD
THE BOSS'S DEFECTION

During the extraction attempt, THE BOSS appeared unexpectedly.

She announced her defection to the Soviet Union and transferred Sokolov into the custody of Colonel Volgin.

The event fundamentally changed the status of the mission and resulted in the United States regarding The Boss as a traitor.

COLONEL VOLGIN

Colonel Volgin arrived at the extraction location with Soviet forces and took possession of Sokolov.

Volgin subsequently used a Davy Crockett tactical nuclear weapon against the OKB-754 research facility.

COLONEL VOLGIN OKB-754
NUCLEAR INCIDENT

The destruction of OKB-754 created an international crisis.

The United States government publicly attributed the nuclear strike to The Boss, reinforcing the perception that she had defected and acted against American interests.

The incident placed severe political pressure on the United States and the Soviet Union.

AFTERMATH

The Virtuous Mission ended without the successful extraction of Sokolov.

The United States subsequently authorized a second operation to recover Sokolov, destroy the Shagohod and eliminate The Boss.

OPERATION SNAKE EATER
HISTORICAL CONSEQUENCES

The failure of the Virtuous Mission directly resulted in the events of Operation Snake Eater.

The apparent defection of The Boss became one of the central political and military issues surrounding the subsequent operation.

The destruction of OKB-754 also demonstrated the international consequences that could result from the Shagohod project and the struggle surrounding Sokolov's research.
