LEGACY

Major Zero's influence extended beyond the events of the Cold War and contributed to the formation of the intelligence infrastructure that would later affect global information control.

His ideological conflict with Big Boss ultimately produced two opposing approaches to the legacy of The Boss.

Zero pursued control through information and centralized systems, while Big Boss pursued military independence and the creation of a world where soldiers could determine their own fate.
