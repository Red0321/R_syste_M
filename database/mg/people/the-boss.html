THE VIRTUOUS MISSION

The Boss was initially assigned to assist in the recovery of Soviet scientist NIKOLAI SOKOLOV .

During the mission, she unexpectedly defected to the Soviet Union and delivered Sokolov into the custody of Colonel Volgin.

The apparent defection resulted in the cancellation of the mission and the immediate deployment of Naked Snake.

OPERATION SNAKE EATER

The United States later authorized OPERATION SNAKE EATER with the objective of recovering Sokolov, destroying the Shagohod and eliminating The Boss.

During the operation, The Boss operated alongside Colonel Volgin and the Cobra Unit while continuing to pursue objectives that were not fully understood by the United States command.

Her actions during this period were subsequently revealed to have been connected to a larger political operation involving the Philosophers.

RELATIONSHIP WITH NAKED SNAKE

The Boss served as Naked Snake's mentor and superior officer.

She trained him in close-quarters combat and passed on a combat philosophy centered around discipline, adaptability and the relationship between soldier and battlefield.

Their relationship became one of the defining elements of the events surrounding Operation Snake Eater.

The Boss's final mission was connected to the activities of THE PHILOSOPHERS , an international organization whose influence extended across multiple governments.

FINAL CONFRONTATION

The final confrontation between The Boss and Naked Snake took place during the closing stages of Operation Snake Eater.

Snake defeated The Boss after a prolonged confrontation.

Her death allowed the operation to reach its conclusion and enabled the United States to publicly maintain the official narrative surrounding her supposed betrayal.

LEGACY

The Boss's influence continued long after her death.

Her teachings, ideology and actions became reference points for several individuals and military organizations involved in later conflicts.

Naked Snake's transformation into BIG BOSS was directly connected to the events surrounding her death.

Her name and legacy would subsequently become associated with competing interpretations of loyalty, patriotism, military identity and the meaning of a soldier's place in the world.
