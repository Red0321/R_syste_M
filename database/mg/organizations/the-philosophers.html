THE PHILOSOPHERS
ORGANIZATION RECORD
CLASSIFICATION SECRET ORGANIZATION
STATUS DISSOLVED
PERIOD 20TH CENTURY
SUMMARY

The Philosophers were a secret organization composed of powerful individuals from the United States, Soviet Union and China.

The organization accumulated enormous financial resources and political influence, allowing its members to manipulate events beyond the control of conventional governments.

Its activities became one of the earliest known foundations of the international power structures that would later influence the history of modern warfare.

ORIGIN

The Philosophers were established by three influential figures who sought to maintain cooperation between their respective nations despite political and ideological differences.

The organization accumulated a vast financial reserve known as the PHILOSOPHERS' LEGACY .

The death of the organization's founders eventually resulted in the fragmentation of the organization and the disappearance of direct control over its resources.

PHILOSOPHERS' LEGACY

The Philosophers accumulated a massive financial reserve intended to guarantee the continued existence and influence of the organization.

After the deaths of its founders, information concerning the location and control of the funds became fragmented.

The recovery of the Philosophers' Legacy became a central objective of multiple intelligence and military operations.

DISSOLUTION

The original structure of the Philosophers eventually ceased to function as a unified organization.

Its remaining resources, information and influence were divided among individuals and organizations competing for control of the Philosophers' Legacy.

The resulting struggle contributed to the formation of several organizations whose activities would dominate subsequent decades.

HISTORICAL CONSEQUENCES

The Philosophers' Legacy became the foundation for several later intelligence and military organizations.

The ideological conflict between MAJOR ZERO and BIG BOSS was influenced by their differing interpretations of The Boss's legacy.

The organizations that emerged from this conflict eventually contributed to the development of the Patriots and the global information-control system.
